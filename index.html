The research behind it

27 Nov 2026

Three.js

WebGPU and TSL, contained

WebGPURenderer renders on WebGPU with automatic WebGL2 fallback, and multiple 2026 guides call it production-ready since r171. The swap alone is not automatically faster: one porting report puts it at about 5 to 10%, and non-instanced many-mesh scenes can be slower. Raw GLSL is ignored on the WebGPU backend, so new shaders are authored in TSL. The verdict: adopt inside one isolated, lazy-loaded scene, never as a whole-system migration. That is exactly the hero above.

Sources: threejs.org/docs/llms.txt · utsubo.com · oflight.co.jp

CSS · Motion

Scroll-driven, no library

Scroll-driven animations became Baseline on 27 Oct 2026, completed by Firefox 159, with Chrome 115 and Safari 26 behind them. They run on the compositor thread at 0 KB. The report's rule is mandatory: the finished state is the default style, so older browsers get the settled page. The progress hairline, the hero parallax, the headline weight shift, and the row reveals on this page all run this way.

Sources: dev.to/richicodes · sparelightstudio.com

Transitions

View Transitions, both kinds

Same-document View Transitions have been Baseline since Oct 2025, and React 19.3 (9 Sep 2026) made the framework <ViewTransition> component stable. Cross-document transitions are still Limited, not Baseline: Chrome and Edge 126+, Safari 18.2+, and Firefox has not shipped them, so unsupported browsers simply navigate normally. The plate switcher above uses the same-document form.

Sources: react.dev · botmonster.com

CSS · Tokens

One gold seed, derived in OKLCH

The report calls the OKLCH token rebuild the single highest-leverage upgrade: one seed colour, with hover, muted, border, and glow variants derived by relative color syntax and color-mix(), perceptually even. Beside it: @property (Baseline since 2024) makes tokens animatable, container queries are widely available, :has() is widely available since Jun 2026, and anchor positioning became Baseline in Jan 2026. All five are live on this page.

Sources: modern-css.com · kylemonti.com

Glass

Liquid Glass discipline, tinted

Apple introduced Liquid Glass on 9 Jun 2025 as a rendered material for the floating navigation layer, not a surface style for content. The documented failure mode is contrast: beta testing found text over glass as low as 1.5:1 against the WCAG 4.5:1 line for body text, and CSS cannot refract. So this page borrows the discipline only: tinted, near-opaque glass on the top bar and hero panel, a gold specular edge, and solid content surfaces everywhere else.

Sources: apple.com/newsroom · liquidglassdesign.com · kube.io

Budgets

Award-level performance budgets

From the research: under 100 draw calls a frame on desktop, under 50 on mobile; hero objects at 50 to 100K triangles, about 500K total; DPR capped at 2, because DPR 3 renders 9x the pixels; at most 3 shadow-casting lights and no shadow maps on mobile; and test sustained load, since thermal throttling can drop 60 fps to 20 fps after about 30 seconds. The fallback stack is non-negotiable: feature-detect first, poster for reduced motion, pause offscreen, content always in the DOM.

Source: github.com/eneryleen/ai-web-design-codex

Case studies

Proof it ships

Bruno Simon's portfolio runs Three.js with TSL and WebGPU with WebGL fallback at 60 fps on a mid-2022 laptop, and took Awwwards Site of the Month in Jan 2026 per the award log. Minh Pham's portfolio is the transferable pattern: WebGL displacement transitions between full-resolution images, the shader working around the image, never degrading it. Lando Norris, Awwwards Site of the Year 2025, pairs bold type with a single neon accent on black, the closest award analogue to gold on ink.

Sources: roughworks.ca · hontran.dev · github.com/hosnainrafi/designgate

Open questions

What the research could not settle

  • WebGPU coverage. Sources report about 80%, about 83 to 84%, and about 87%. Directionally four-fifths plus with fallback required, but no single settled figure was verified against caniuse directly.
  • Motion's current version. 2026 sources variously report v12.42.2 (Jun), 13.4.0 (Sep), and 14.13.0 (Nov). The API and positioning are consistent; the exact version is unverified, and this build ships no animation library at all.
  • field-sizing in Firefox. Reported as both 121 and 152; the Baseline Jun 2026 status is consistent across sources.
  • Cross-document View Transitions in Firefox 162, listed by one MDN-derived source as due 8 Dec 2026: forthcoming, not shipped. Treat as a future enhancement only.
  • Verification limits. No live-browser verification was done in the research run; every source was read via web index on 27 Nov 2026, and award claims come from the cited case-study and award-log pages, not from Awwwards directly.

Release notes — Full Build v2

The whole page at full throttle, production-ready for Cloudflare Pages. Opened from the Live chip or the plate bar. The previous hub is backed up for revert — see the Deploy section at the foot of the page.

  • Whole page, not hero-only. v1 treated the hero. v2 extends the treatment to every entry, system card, shelf card and section head: gold sheen sweeps, specular top lines, lifts, and arrow cues on the index rows.
  • Three.js hero. A gold dust field in a custom shader, aperture rings with procedural grain, and a fresnel lens glow: 5 draw calls. WebGPU is detected and reported in the hero chip; rendering falls back to WebGL2 automatically, DPR is capped at 2 with a frame-rate governor, the scene lazy loads, pauses offscreen, and the photograph is the permanent poster fallback.
  • TSL-style shaders. The dust shader is composed from small node functions in script (add, multiply, sine, smoothstep) that compile to GLSL, the TSL authoring shape, since raw GLSL is what the WebGPU backend leaves behind.
  • OKLCH tokens in @layer. Every gold derives from the #c9a227 seed by relative color and color-mix, inside a declared layer order: reset, tokens, base, components, utilities.
  • Scroll-driven motion. Progress, parallax, reveals, and headline weight run on scroll and view timelines behind @supports, with the finished state as the default and a script fallback for reveals.
  • View transitions. The plate switcher rearranges the index inside a same-document view transition, with shared names on the plate heads, at 240 ms.
  • @property everywhere it pays. Registered properties animate the three budget rings, the hero headline weight axis, and the gold sheens across panels, entries and cards.
  • Kinetic type, one headline only. The masthead is a variable serif: letters fatten near the pointer, and the whole word gains weight as you start to scroll. Body text never animates.
  • Tinted liquid glass. Glass only on chrome: the top bar, the hero panel, the chips, and this sheet. Near-opaque ink tint, specular gold edge, blur removed under reduced transparency.
  • Deployment & revert. Deploy this build to the Cloudflare Pages northbrand project as its root index.html. Revert by redeploying the backed-up 2026-11-25 Aperture Index file, or by rolling back to the previous production deployment in the Cloudflare dashboard.
